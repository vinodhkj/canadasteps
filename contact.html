Contact CanadaSteps

Send a correction, suggestion, or site-related question.

CanadaSteps welcomes corrections and suggestions that make the guides clearer, safer, and more useful for newcomers. Please do not send private documents, identification numbers, immigration files, tax records, or banking details.

Before you contact CanadaSteps: CanadaSteps can review site feedback, broken links, unclear wording, or general content suggestions. It cannot assess your personal eligibility, review your application, calculate your taxes, or provide legal, immigration, tax, or financial advice.

01 Best way to reach us

Email: getgroundpages@gmail.com

Use this email for correction requests, broken links, source updates, accessibility issues, or suggestions for new guides.

02 What to include

  • Page name or URL: Tell us which CanadaSteps page you are referring to.
  • The issue: Explain what seems outdated, unclear, broken, or missing.
  • Official source, if available: Include a government, CRA, Service Canada, provincial, school, or institutional link that supports the correction.
  • Keep it non-private: Do not send passport numbers, SINs, UCI numbers, application numbers, tax slips, bank statements, or personal documents.

03 What CanadaSteps cannot do

No personal immigration advice

CanadaSteps cannot decide whether you qualify for a permit, PR pathway, work authorization, or family application.

No tax or financial advice

CanadaSteps cannot calculate your tax filing position, investment room, benefit eligibility, or banking decision for your personal case.

No government support desk

CanadaSteps is not IRCC, CRA, Service Canada, a province, a school, or a bank. For account or application issues, contact the relevant official organization.

No emergency support

CanadaSteps is not monitored for urgent issues. Use official emergency, legal, government, or professional support channels when needed.
