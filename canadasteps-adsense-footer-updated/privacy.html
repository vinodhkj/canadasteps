Privacy Policy

This page explains what CanadaSteps may collect, how it is used, and how advertising and analytics tools may use cookies or similar technologies.

Last reviewed: July 2026
Applies to: canadasteps.ca
Plain-language policy
CanadaSteps is an independent educational guide site. It is not affiliated with IRCC, CRA, Service Canada, any provincial government, or the Government of Canada.

01Information you provide

CanadaSteps is designed as a public information website. Most pages can be used without creating an account or submitting personal information.

If you contact CanadaSteps through a contact form, email link, or similar feature, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, message content, and any details you include in your request.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information, immigration documents, passport numbers, SIN numbers, banking details, medical information, or other confidential records through this website.

02Analytics and site usage data

CanadaSteps may use analytics tools, including Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the site and to improve page quality, navigation, and content coverage.

Analytics tools may collect information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, pages visited, referring pages, interaction events, and general usage patterns. This information is used in aggregated form and is not meant to identify individual visitors.

03Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. CanadaSteps and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics, security, remembering preferences, measuring page performance, and advertising if ads are enabled.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Some site features or third-party services may not work the same way if cookies are blocked.

04Advertising and Google AdSense

CanadaSteps may display advertising through Google AdSense or other advertising partners. If ads are enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to CanadaSteps or other websites.

Google advertising cookies: Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

Third-party vendors: Third parties may place and read cookies on your browser, use web beacons, or use IP addresses and similar technologies as a result of ad serving on this website.

Personalized ads: Some ads may be personalized based on previous activity, depending on your settings, region, and applicable law.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use Google services at Google’s partner sites policy page.

You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You may also visit AboutAds.info to learn about other advertising choices.

05Consent messages

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may see a cookie or consent message before certain advertising or analytics features are used. This may apply to visitors in regions such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or other jurisdictions with consent requirements.

06External links

CanadaSteps links to official government, institutional, and other third-party websites. External websites have their own privacy policies, terms, and content standards. CanadaSteps is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

07How information is used

Information collected through this website may be used to:

  • operate and maintain the website;
  • understand which pages are useful to visitors;
  • improve guides, tools, navigation, and accessibility;
  • respond to messages sent by visitors;
  • protect the site from spam, abuse, or technical issues;
  • support advertising measurement and delivery if ads are enabled.

08Data retention

CanadaSteps keeps information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Analytics and advertising providers may maintain their own retention practices.

09Children’s privacy

CanadaSteps is intended for general audiences seeking newcomer information. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

10Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, use the contact method listed on the Contact page.

11Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the site changes, including if new tools, forms, analytics features, or advertising services are added. The “Last reviewed” date above will be updated when meaningful changes are made.
